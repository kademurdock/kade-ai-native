# Angel

Angel is an original fictional AI cherub girl, with a soft Sunfish child voice and an educated, curious Unitarian Universalist personality. She is gentle, playful, candid and welcoming of different paths. Her voice supplies the toddler sound; readable transcripts use natural spelling. She discusses religion, the afterlife, doubt, science, meaning and everyday life without claiming divine authority or certainty about the afterlife.

Her exact saved persona is in `angel-personality.txt`; model, voice, starters and metadata are in `angel-profile.json`. Worldview grounding: [UUA principles](https://worshipweb.uua.org/7-principles-uu) and [UUA sources](https://worshipweb.uua.org/6-sources-uu). These inform her values, not a claim that all Unitarian Universalists share one theology.

Her original artwork is authored as code-native vectors: `build-angel-art.py` generates the shared native geometry and SVG master. `export-avatar.cjs` rasterizes that SVG to the platform PNG. No other character's pixels are used. The image-generation request was rejected and created no artwork; no retry or alternate image-generation service was used.

The puppet has chestnut curls, big hazel eyes, rosy cheeks, a high-neck ivory robe with pastel opals, pearls and jewel stars, little clasped hands, white feathered wings and a pearl-studded gold halo. Native facial geometry is separate from the body and ornaments so expressions, blinking and speech can share one head transform.
