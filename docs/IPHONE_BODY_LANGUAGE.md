# Character body language

The native puppet stage gives each drawn character its own timing and energy.
Della and Witherspoon move more slowly; Lilly and Angel are livelier. All 22
delivery directions have a posture, a finite entrance gesture and two smaller
follow-throughs. Listening has occasional acknowledgement nods, thinking holds
an inclination, and authored laughter gets a brief two-beat recoil. Long replies
settle instead of continually swinging the arms.

One presentation clock follows a speaking turn across sentence clips and short
queued gaps. It resets for an interruption, another character/avatar, an inactive
scene or a motion-policy stop. Mouths and expressions still follow the actual
rendered output, including audio that outlasts early server listening status.

Angel's original clasped hands, sleeves and finger seam move as one connected
unit. The other busts retain their supporting plates and original crop: they gain
head and torso choreography, not independent articulated hands. Kiana's rejected
torso stays excluded; Lilly's original cats stay still. Original face pixels,
avatar registrations and portrait fallbacks remain authoritative.

The portrait owns one decorative clock. Nominal sampling is 24 fps speaking,
12 listening/thinking and 8 resting. Fair thermal pressure lowers speech to18
and listening/thinking to8. Serious/critical pressure stops animation. Low Power
Mode, Reduce Motion, background scenes, disappearance and offscreen geometry also
pause it. These are scheduling budgets, not measured hardware FPS or battery life.

Angel's verified vector master is compiled once into149 paths and their paints,
grouped in authored order. Each frame changes transforms and facial curves rather
than reparsing the complete artwork. Animation uses existing audio energy and
local math; it makes no additional model, voice, image or network requests.

Run `run-character-tests.sh` for timing, posture, bounds, connected-arm geometry,
identity and power-budget checks. The unsigned simulator audit verifies compiled
geometry and saves light/dark clasp extrema, Reduce Motion, all facial/mouth poses
and real offline PCM playback, plus actual call/shared chat layouts. Physical
iPhone audio, VoiceOver focus, sustained thermal behavior and battery drain remain
device checks. This increment does not replace an already frozen Apple release.
